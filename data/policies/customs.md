# Customs Documentation Policy

## Required documents for international shipments
- Commercial invoice with HS codes, value, currency and origin of goods.
- Packing list.
- Bill of lading (sea) or CMR (road).
- For shipments to non-EU destinations: export declaration.

## Customs hold
- A shipment goes to "customs_hold" when documents are missing, inconsistent or when selected for inspection.
- Most common cause: commercial invoice without HS codes or with a value mismatch.
- Resolution: the customer or their broker uploads a corrected document; customs typically releases within 1–3 business days after a complete document set is received.
- Storage fees may apply after 5 business days at the port terminal.

## Who can do what
- Customers may be told which document is missing and how to submit it.
- Only staff roles may see broker contact details and internal customs notes.
