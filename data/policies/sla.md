# Delivery SLA Policy (Meridian Freight)

## Service targets
- Parcel (LastMile Go): delivery within 1 business day inside the Netherlands once the shipment is out for delivery.
- Road freight (Continental Road Express): 2 business days NL→DE, 3 business days NL→FR from pickup.
- Sea freight (OceanLine Global): transit time is indicative; port congestion and customs inspection are excluded from SLA measurement.

## Delay handling
- A shipment is "delayed" when the carrier reports a revised ETA later than the original ETA.
- Delays caused by weather, road closures or strikes are classified as force majeure: no service credit applies, but customers must be proactively informed.
- Delays caused by Meridian or its carrier (missed pickup, misrouting) qualify for a service credit of 10% of the freight charge for each full business day of delay, capped at 50%.

## Communication
- Customers must be notified of any delay longer than 12 hours.
- Support agents may offer a carrier change only through the carrier-change request workflow, which requires operations manager approval.
